Camps & Activities

Camp isn't just for summer — here's everything on the books this year, from family campouts to a snowy day camp.

Fall Day Camp

The fall season is a great time to get kids excited about camping and the outdoors! Fall Day Camp is a half-day opportunity for all youth and their families to kick off the school year — newly registered Cub Scouts and their families and friends get to participate in activities and games while learning about the year-round camping opportunities Scouting offers.

BB Gun Shooting Archery Climbing Pedal Carts Team Building Games Carnival Games STEM Hiking Popcorn
  • When: Sunday, October 4, 2026 · 1:00 PM – 4:30 PM (Session 4)
  • Where: Kiwanis Scout Camp
  • Registration opens: September 1
  • Our pack has chosen this session, but you're free to pick another date, session, or location if it works better for your family.

Spooky-Ree

Get ready for a thrilling adventure with your family and friends at our Spooky-Ree Fall Camporee! This unique experience creates lifelong memories and valuable skills for elementary-aged youth — a chance to challenge themselves, build confidence, and develop new friendships through camping, games, and outdoor skills, all in a safe and supportive environment.

BB Gun Shooting Archery Tent Camping Campfires S'mores Hayride Pedal Carts Hiking Board Games Crafts Trunk or Treat Camp Store
  • When: October 23–25, 2026 (overnight) · day-only option on October 24
  • Where: Kiwanis Scout Camp
  • Registration opens: September 1
  • Our pack has chosen this date, but Spooky-Ree is also offered at Phillippo Scout Camp and Stearns Scout Camp — pick whichever date or location works best for your family.

Base Camp Lock-In

Pack as much adventure as you can into a 12-hour visit to Base Camp. Programs run 6:30–10:00 PM with activities like rock climbing and archery, a pizza break halfway through, then a movie on the big screen. After the movie, scouts can head to bed — or stay up all night for the full experience.
