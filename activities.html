Camps & Activities

Camp isn't just for summer — here's everything on the books this year, from family campouts to a snowy day camp.

Family Camp

A weekend of family camping with pack activities and free time. Families are welcome to stay one or both nights, or just come for Saturday fun.

  • When: June 12 (6:00 PM) – June 14 (11:00 AM)
  • Where: Lake Rebecca State Park, Sarah Creek Group Camp
  • Cost: $20 per family (covers food)
  • Contact: Ryan Bradley — family_accounts@icloud.com
  • Meals provided: Fri campfire snacks · Sat breakfast/lunch/dinner/snacks · Sun breakfast
  • No pets or alcohol permitted. One Google Form needed per family.

Base Camp Lock-In

Pack as much adventure as you can into a 12-hour visit to Base Camp. Programs run 6:30–10:00 PM with activities like rock climbing and archery, a pizza break halfway through, then a movie on the big screen. After the movie, scouts can head to bed — or stay up all night for the full experience.

  • When: March 6–7 (6:30 PM – 8:00 AM)
  • Where: Base Camp, 6202 Bloomington Rd, Fort Snelling, MN 55111
  • More information to come.
