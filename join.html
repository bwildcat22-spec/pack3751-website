How To Join

Two Tuesdays. Two Ways to Join In.

Same time, same place, one week apart — pick one or come to both.

Meet our pack leaders, get the full scoop on Cub Scouting, and ask every question you've got — no pressure, just answers.

The following Tuesday, sit in on a real den meeting and see the small-group setting where your scout will make friends, earn badges, and have fun each week.
