Our Pack

A place for the whole family to grow together.

Our Cub Scout Pack is a welcoming community for all families, embracing both boys and girls in an adventure-filled program designed for kids in kindergarten through fifth grade. Whether your child attends a public, private, or homeschool, they're welcome here.

We're sponsored by St. Joseph's Parish Community in New Hope, MN, but we welcome all individuals of any religious preference or belief — because Scouting is for everyone.
